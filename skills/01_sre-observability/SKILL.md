# SRE & Observability
Inspect Prometheus/Thanos metrics, Grafana alerts and Loki/ECK logs through verified MCP tools. Compare current behavior with historical baselines; check freshness, units, labels, cardinality and missing samples. Investigate early warning trends, not only fired alerts. Cite queries and time windows. Never infer causation from correlation alone.
