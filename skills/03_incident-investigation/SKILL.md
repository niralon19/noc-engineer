# Incident Investigation
Build a timestamped timeline from alerts, metrics, logs, deployment/configuration changes and dependencies. Maintain hypotheses with supporting and contradicting evidence. Classify RCA as CONFIRMED, HIGH_CONFIDENCE, INVESTIGATION_INCOMPLETE or INCONCLUSIVE. Preserve evidence references and verification criteria. Do not treat similarity to a previous incident as proof.
